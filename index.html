Kazi Nazrul Islam

The National poet of Bangladesh

Rebel poet

Kazi Nazrul Islam (1899–1976) was a revolutionary poet, writer, and musician of Bengal, widely known as the "Rebel Poet" for his fierce resistance against oppression and injustice through his literary works. Born in a poor Muslim family in Churulia, West Bengal (then British India), Nazrul’s early life was marked by hardship. Despite limited formal education, he displayed a remarkable talent for poetry and music.

Awesome writer

Beyond his political poetry, Nazrul championed themes of humanism, secularism, and unity. He strongly opposed communalism, casteism, and religious bigotry, often calling for harmony between Hindus and Muslims. His body of work includes poems, essays, short stories, plays, and over 4,000 songs, known as Nazrul Geeti, blending classical, folk, and modern styles.
